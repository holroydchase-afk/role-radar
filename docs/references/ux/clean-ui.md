## CLEAN-1 White space
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://medium.com/@opebamiduro/5-rules-of-clean-ui-design-that-actually-work-8c95395c2323
- tags: clean, white space, spacing, crowded, breathing room, focus
Give content room to breathe. A crowded screen feels stressful; a roomy one invites focus and reads easily. Check: related things sit close, unrelated things clearly apart, nothing touches its container's edge, and the first screen is not packed (see C2, V3).

## CLEAN-2 One or two fonts
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://medium.com/@opebamiduro/5-rules-of-clean-ui-design-that-actually-work-8c95395c2323
- tags: clean, fonts, typography, families, headings, body
Type sets the tone, and more than two fonts gets messy fast. Use one font for headings and one for body (one family for both is fine). Check: count the families on the screen; more than two is a fail, with a mono family allowed only for code, commands, paths and table numbers (V2, V11).

## CLEAN-3 A limited color palette
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://medium.com/@opebamiduro/5-rules-of-clean-ui-design-that-actually-work-8c95395c2323
- tags: clean, color, palette, primary, secondary, neutral
Three colors is often enough: a primary, a secondary and a neutral. That keeps a design cohesive and intentional; too many colors feel chaotic and unprofessional. Check: name the primary, secondary and neutral on the screen; any further hue must be a status color that means status only (V6).

## CLEAN-4 Alignment and a grid
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://medium.com/@opebamiduro/5-rules-of-clean-ui-design-that-actually-work-8c95395c2323
- tags: clean, alignment, grid, structure, misaligned
A grid gives structure and an organized feel. Misaligned text, buttons or icons break the visual flow and confuse people. Check: things share left edges, labels and values line up, icons sit centered with their text, and a row of items shares a baseline (V4).

## CLEAN-5 Consistency
- strength: hard
- status: fetched
- source: https://medium.com/@opebamiduro/5-rules-of-clean-ui-design-that-actually-work-8c95395c2323
- tags: clean, consistency, padding, font sizes, components, patterns
Use the same padding, font sizes, colors and component styles across the product; the more consistent it is, the easier it is to understand and move through. Check: like things look and behave alike on this screen and against the product's other screens (D6, V3, V5).
